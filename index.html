PROFILE

About

A soil scientist focused on turning organic residues and advanced soil amendments into practical tools for healthier, more resilient agricultural systems.

Dr. Qayyum earned his Ph.D. in Agriculture from Justus Liebig University Giessen, Germany, in 2012, studying stabilization of soil organic matter using biochars. He joined Bahauddin Zakariya University the same year and has built an internationally connected research programme spanning soil carbon, biochar, nutrient dynamics, heavy-metal remediation, nanoparticles and climate-resilient soil management.

RESEARCH

Research themes

01

Biochar & Circular Resources

Production, characterization, modification and agronomic use of biochars from crop residues, manure and sewage sludge.

02

Soil Carbon & Climate

Carbon stabilization, mineralization, greenhouse-gas mitigation and soil organic matter management.

03

Contaminated Soil Remediation

Cadmium, lead and other metal interactions, immobilization strategies and reduction of plant-uptake risk.

04

Nutrient Dynamics

Phosphorus sorption and availability, nitrogen retention, potassium efficiency and sustainable soil fertility.

05

Nanomaterials & Soil

Engineered biochars and nanoparticle–soil–plant interactions for nutrient management and stress mitigation.

06

Climate-Resilient Agriculture

Salinity, drought, microplastics, residue management and soil-water strategies for resilient cropping systems.
