PROFILE

About

A soil scientist focused on turning organic residues and advanced soil amendments into practical tools for healthier, more resilient agricultural systems.

Dr. Qayyum earned his Ph.D. in Agriculture from Justus Liebig University Giessen, Germany, in 2012, studying stabilization of soil organic matter using biochars. He joined Bahauddin Zakariya University the same year and has built an internationally connected research programme spanning soil carbon, biochar, nutrient dynamics, heavy-metal remediation, nanoparticles and climate-resilient soil management.

RESEARCH BLOG & COMMUNITY

Research notes

9 NOVEMBER 2026 · SOIL CHARACTERIZATION

What can the same soil sample tell an agronomist and an engineer?

Start with the synthetic Punjab alluvial sample in TerraWise AI. The sand, silt, and clay fractions give a USDA texture of loam. The sieve and Atterberg results support a preliminary USCS screen of CL. In a teaching lab, ask students to list the inputs behind each result and flag any missing measurements before interpreting the sample.

For a building project, the next questions concern the depth and continuity of the layer, groundwater, and which strength and settlement measurements the engineer needs. Keep the raw measurements, method, and specimen ID with the classification so others can review the result.
